03

Promo plan

How the numbers work. Every promo price is your listing price less a discount, where the discount is the smaller of what the sale moment normally runs at and your own ceiling — then rounded to a ₱x9 ending and never taken above list. Bundle and multi-buy figures are built from the component prices you enter. Voucher, free-shipping and gift thresholds are set from the average price of the SKUs in your list, so they only pay out once a buyer adds more than one item. These are selling-price mechanics, not a profit model — sense-check the depth against your own costs before you publish, and confirm current mechanics and campaign requirements in each Seller Centre before submitting.
